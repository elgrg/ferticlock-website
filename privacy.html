Privacy policy

FertiClock is a research project from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA). This page explains what we do with the information you give us through our waitlist.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • That you agreed to receive emails from us, the date, and which version of the consent text you agreed to.
  • The language of the page you signed up from.

We do not ask for any health information. The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

Why we collect it

Only to send you news about FertiClock: the test, early studies you could take part in, and our launch. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

Where it is stored and who can see it

Sign-ups are stored in a Google Sheet that only the FertiClock team can open. Google acts as our processor and may process data outside the European Union under its standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share your email with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until the project ends, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time, and ask to see, correct or delete your data, by writing to [TEAM EMAIL]. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

FertiClock is in research and development and is not yet available. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or predict fertility or the chance of conception. Always consult a healthcare professional.
